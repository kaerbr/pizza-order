Anatomy

  1. 01

    Logo & naming

    Mark, NERO, the night and the house. Kept exactly as it was — this is the hand-made part.

  2. 02

    Who it is for

    A lowercase “for”, the name, the seat. No guest number, no formality.

  3. 03

    Ticket line

    Order number and oven-ready time on one quiet line at 2.2 mm.

  4. 04

    The pizza

    Title case at 4.2 mm — the biggest words after the logo. Size, dough and oven underneath.

  5. 05

    Ingredients

    One per line, lowercase, 2.4 mm. This is the reason the slip exists; give it the room.

  6. 06

    Extras & notes

    Additions are bold, removals and cuts follow. Short enough to read at the bench.

  7. 07

    Your number

    9 mm, unboxed, with a nudge. Loud without looking like a tax form.

  8. 08

    Footer

    Thanks, eat-it-hot, and the party line. Three short lines, nothing legal.
